The Mission

Why 100km
a Day?

Because men are dying. Not from the desert heat. Not from the kilometres. They're dying because they don't talk. Men's mental health is in crisis across Australia — and the silence is the killer.

Running 4,000km from Perth to Sydney — unsupported, pushing a 45kg buggy — isn't a stunt. It's a statement. If one person can push that far without stopping, we can all take one step toward a conversation we've been avoiding.

"Every km I run is a km I'm not staying silent. Every donation gets a man into a room where it's safe to speak."

Men's Talk provides real, accessible mental health support for men across Australia. Your donation funds counselling, peer support programs, and the kind of conversations that save lives. The $10,000 target isn't arbitrary — it's the number that funds meaningful change.

The buggy, the solar gear, the drone footage — none of it matters unless people see it, share it, and decide this mission is worth backing. Follow along. Tell someone. And if you can, donate.
