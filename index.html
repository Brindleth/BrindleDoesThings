The Man Behind the Mission

From England
to the Outback:
Why I'm Running.

I am a UK-trained and qualified Electrical & Electronic Engineer who moved to Australia to escape a cycle of addiction that nearly cost me my life.

After arriving, I faced immediate hardship — living in my car for three months while working Uber Eats just to survive. No safety net. No support crew. Sound familiar?

"Having lost close friends and family to addiction and suicide, I realised I wanted to use my struggle to show other men that you can always keep pushing forward — no matter how dark it gets."

This run isn't about endurance records. It's about proof. Proof that the darkest chapter of your life doesn't have to be the last one. Every kilometre I push that buggy across the outback is a kilometre I didn't give up.

Men's Talk exists to create the rooms where those conversations happen safely. Follow along. Tell someone. And if you can, donate.

Who Gets

Men's Talk — Men's Mental Health Australia

Men's Talk provides real, accessible mental health support for men across Australia — counselling, peer programs, and conversations that save lives.

75% of suicides in Australia are men. Men are far less likely to seek help. This fundraiser turns Matthew's lived experience into direct action.

Your donation goes 100% to the charity. Not one cent of this fund covers the buggy, shoes, or food.
