The Person Behind the Build

From England
to the Outback:
Why I'm Building.

I am a UK-trained and qualified Electrical & Electronic Engineer who moved to Australia to rebuild — physically, mentally and technically — after coming through recovery from addiction.

After arriving, I faced immediate hardship: living in my car for three months while working delivery jobs just to survive. No safety net. No support crew. That stretch taught me exactly how much you can engineer your way out of, if you're willing to keep showing up — and how easily men end up isolated long before anyone notices.

"This project is recovery, endurance and engineering welded into one 4,000km object. The build is mine. The awareness — men's mental health, suicide prevention, speaking up before crisis point — is the reason it's being filmed."

This expedition isn't about a distance record. It's about building something real with my own hands, then pointing it at the horizon and seeing if it makes it. Every weld, every wiring loom, every kilometre is documented — from workshop to finish line.

The bigger reason behind it is simple: men's mental health and suicide awareness. If the build or the drive gets one person to speak up before things reach crisis point, the whole thing was worth welding. Follow along, share the channel, and if you can, donate to the cause or support the build.
